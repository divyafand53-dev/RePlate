Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Replate ensure the safety of donated food?

Every food listing requires the donor to specify preparation time, holding conditions, and an expiry consumption deadline. Listings automatically update through 🟢 Fresh, 🟡 Collect Soon, and 🔴 Urgent stages. Expired listings are automatically deactivated.

Is Replate free to use for NGOs and orphanages?

Yes, 100% free. Replate is dedicated to social welfare and food-waste reduction. Verified orphanages, old age homes, and community langars receive food donations at zero cost.

Who handles the transportation and pickup of food?

Our platform connects registered volunteers who transport the food in thermal bags or boxes. Alternatively, nearby NGOs with their own transport vehicles can collect directly using our secure OTP verification system.
